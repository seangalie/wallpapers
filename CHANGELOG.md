# Changelog

Notable changes to Sean's Wallpaper Archive are documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and releases use [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/) as it applies to
a collection: adding wallpapers is a minor release, corrections are a patch,
and moving or renaming published paths or downloads is a major release.

## [3.2.0] - 2026-10-12

639 wallpapers join the archive, bringing it to 2,247 images: 79 for phones,
546 for desktops, 13 ultrawide, and one square. The gallery site can now list
the newest wallpapers first, keep your favorites, and open on wallpapers that
suit your device. Download names are unchanged.

### Added

- **Newest first and favorites on the gallery site:** sort by **Newest**
  under Browse to see the latest additions first, and look for the **New**
  badge on wallpapers added in the last 30 days. Each wallpaper's details
  now show the date it was added. Select the heart on any wallpaper to save
  it as a favorite, then choose **Favorites** under Browse to see only those.
  Favorites are kept in your browser, so they stay on that device.
- **33 phone wallpapers** from my old laptop, all enlarged and listed under
  "Modified images" in [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md):
  - graphic jokes and cartoons, including "Follow the leader", a nature
    speedometer, a "This is Sparta" caution sign, the Flash getting a speeding
    ticket, Calvin and Hobbes, Snoopy, and Homer Simpson
  - film, TV, and game designs: Matrix code, Tron, Interstellar's TARS,
    Breaking Bad, The Nightmare Before Christmas, Portal, Deus Ex, Space
    Invaders, and two Star Wars posters
  - abstract light, line, and geometric designs in `mobile/abstract`
  - a tilt-shift village below a mesa and a smoking skull
- **46 more phone wallpapers** from my older wallpaper folders, all enlarged
  and listed under "Modified images" in [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md):
  - painted walker mechs, airships, and robots in `mobile/scifi`
  - dark knights, horned creatures, and painted landscapes in `mobile/fantasy`
  - Archer, Firefly, Evangelion, and Attack on Titan designs in
    `mobile/pop-culture`, and XCOM, Portal, and BioShock in `mobile/gaming`
  - space art, low-poly abstracts, a misty forest, and a pixel-art city at
    night
- **419 wallpapers** from an old wallpaper collection, 418 for desktops and
  one square, most at 1920×1080 or larger:
  - 94 landscapes, seascapes, and nature close-ups in `desktops/outdoors`
  - 71 game wallpapers in `desktops/gaming`, including Fallout, Portal,
    BioShock, Team Fortress 2, Mario, Pokémon, Half-Life, and Mass Effect
  - painted cities, ruins, and spaceships in `desktops/scifi` and
    `desktops/fantasy`, plus art, abstract, and space wallpapers
  - circuit boards, chips, and hard drives in `desktops/tech`, cars in
    `desktops/cars`, and a square circuit-board close-up in a new
    `square/tech` category
  - four were too small for today's screens, so they were enlarged and
    listed under "Modified images" in [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md)
- **Credits:** Dragonhatch Productions is credited for
  `sigantium-robed-figure`, from the copyright line on the image, and 26 of

  brand whose signature or mark they carry, including Noah Bradley, Android
  Jones, Raphael Lacoste, Digital Blasphemy, Vladstudio, and Bill Watterson.

- **141 more wallpapers** from four themed collections:
  - 57 Star Wars wallpapers in `desktops/star-wars`, from painted battles
    and LEGO stormtroopers to brand parodies like "Jedi" in Nike's style
  - 22 planets, nebulas, and galaxies in `desktops/space`
  - 13 ultrawide wallpapers: 11 space renders in `ultrawide/space` and two
    Mass Effect scenes in `ultrawide/gaming`
  - comic and game favourites, including Batman, Deadpool, the Lord of the
    Rings, StarCraft II, Halo, Dead Space, Diablo III, and Mass Effect
  - one was too small for today's screens, so it was enlarged and listed
    under "Modified images" in [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md)
- **Credits:** Zach Bush, Sam Spratt, John Aslarona, Syanart, Blizzard

### Changed

- **The gallery site opens on wallpapers for your device:** phone wallpapers
  on a phone, and desktop wallpapers on a computer or tablet, still in a
  shuffled mix. Choose another format, or **All formats**, under Browse to
  see the rest. Links with filters in them open exactly as shared.

  `desktops/space/nebula-purple.jpg` and `desktops/outdoors/seascape.jpg`

## [3.0.0] - 2026-10-11

The archive gets a gallery site, 102 new wallpapers, and sharper copies of
three more, bringing it to 1,541 images. This is a major release because 25
wallpapers were removed: if you link to or script any of them, see
**Removed** below.

### Added

- **A gallery site at <https://seangalie.github.io/wallpapers/>:** browse the
  whole archive as previews, filter by display format and category, search by
  name, and download any single wallpaper at full size. Links to a filtered
  view or a single wallpaper can be bookmarked and shared. The site updates
  itself whenever the collection changes. It opens in a shuffled order, so
  each visit shows a different mix, and sorts A to Z once you pick a format or
  category or search; switch between Shuffle and A to Z under Browse.
- **102 wallpapers**, bringing the archive to 1,541 images:
  - 13 Princeton University campus photos in `desktops/urban`, from the
    university's Zoom backgrounds
  - 41 phone wallpapers across `mobile`, including mech and sci-fi paintings,
    Futurama and Half-Life posters, pixel art, and outdoor scenes
  - 46 desktop wallpapers, including LEGO Star Wars scenes, Minecraft,
    retro gaming jokes, quotes in `desktops/inspiration`, and three more of
    my own photographs of Lebanon Lake in `desktops/original`
  - the original colors of two Gruvbox paintings already in the archive:
    `child-and-robots-lakeshore` and `robot-in-thicket` in `desktops/scifi`
  - twenty images that were too small for today's screens, enlarged and
    listed under "Modified images" in [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md)
- **Credits:** Daniel Boud is credited for a LEGO clone troopers photo whose
  metadata names him, and the Princeton photos are credited to the
  university.

### Changed

- **Sharper copies of three wallpapers:** `desktops/gaming/tetris.jpg`,
  `desktops/scifi/cyberpunk-city.jpg` (now without a watermark), and
  `desktops/space/rocket-launch-tiltshift.jpg` are replaced by
  higher-resolution versions at the same paths.

### Removed

- **25 wallpapers that no longer fit the collection:** the 23 Omarchy theme
  wallpapers in `desktops/themes` (names containing `_omarchy` or `_oma`) and
  the two `alpha-omega-crown-of-thorns` images in `desktops/art`. Links to
  these files no longer work, and they are gone from the release ZIPs.

## [2.0.0] - 2026-10-07

180 Gruvbox wallpapers join the archive, bringing it to 1,464 images. This is
a major release because the desktop downloads changed: the collection outgrew
GitHub's limit for a single release file, so `wallpapers-desktops.zip` is now
two parts. If you link to or script that download, see **Changed** below.

### Added

- **180 Gruvbox wallpapers:** 171 desktop, 6 ultrawide, and 3 dual-monitor
  images recolored in the warm [Gruvbox](https://github.com/morhetz/gruvbox)
  palette, sorted by subject like the rest of the archive. Their names end in
  `-gruvbox`, or `-gruvbox-light` for the light variant, so they are easy to
  find. Highlights:
  - painted sci-fi countryside, machines, and wrecks in `desktops/scifi`
  - dinosaurs, pterosaurs, and giant creatures in `desktops/fantasy`
  - Linux and NixOS logos with retro stripes in `desktops/tech`
  - Black Mesa, City 17, Doom Eternal, Firewatch, and Red Dead Redemption 2
    scenes in `desktops/gaming`
  - forests, lakes, and mountains in `desktops/outdoors`
  - Gruvbox versions of three wallpapers already in the archive:
    `astronaut-station`, `drawn-spaceship-deck`, and `firewatch-wide-lookout`
  - thirteen images that were too small for today's screens, enlarged and
    listed under "Modified images" in [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md)
- **Credits:** Simon Stålenhag is credited for four paintings that carry his
  signature or web address, and Ahmed Mostafa for an astronaut scene from
  Desktopography.

### Changed

- **Desktop ZIP downloads come in parts.** The desktop collection has grown
  past what GitHub allows in one release download, so `wallpapers-desktops.zip`
  is replaced by `wallpapers-desktops-part-1.zip` and
  `wallpapers-desktops-part-2.zip`. Each part keeps whole categories together:
  part 1 has `abstract` through `outdoors` (about 1.3 GB), and part 2 has
  `pop-culture` through `urban` (about 0.9 GB). Download both for every
  desktop image, or pick a single category from the per-category ZIPs, which
  are unchanged. As the collection grows, a category may move to the other
  part, or a third part may be added.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-03

The first public release of Sean's Wallpaper Archive: 1,250 wallpapers for
single desktops, ultrawide screens, two- and three-monitor setups, phones, and
square displays, gathered from all over the internet and now easy to share.

### Added

- **The collection:** 1,250 wallpapers in six display formats, with 964
  desktop, 65 ultrawide, 108 dual-monitor, 47 triple-monitor, 58 mobile, and 8
  square images, sorted into 21 categories from `abstract` to `urban`.
- **ZIP downloads** with every release: one ZIP per display format, such as
  `wallpapers-desktops.zip` with all 964 desktop images (about 1.8 GB), and one
  per desktop category, such as `wallpapers-desktops-space.zip`. Every ZIP
  includes `LICENSE.txt` explaining the image rights, and `SHA256SUMS.txt`
  lists a checksum for each ZIP.
- **Browsing guides:** a README table linking every category in every format,
  the common resolutions for each format, and an organization guide that
  explains the categories, filenames, and how to choose a format for your
  screen or monitor layout.
- **Credits:** `CREDITS.md` names known photographers, artists, and sources,
  with the evidence for each credit. If you recognize an uncredited image,
  please open an issue.
- **Sean's own photographs** of the New Jersey Pine Barrens, in
  `desktops/original`, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial
  4.0: share and adapt them for non-commercial purposes with credit.
- **Ways to contribute:** guides and issue forms for suggesting wallpapers,
  correcting names or credits, and asking questions, plus a code of conduct.

### Changed

For anyone who used Sean's previous private wallpaper archive (release
`v2026.09`):

- The `desktop` folder is now `desktops`, and `source` is now `square`.
- About 190 images moved to a better-fitting category. Most of the old `art`
  category moved into subject categories such as `outdoors`, `abstract`,
  `urban`, and `space`, and new `abstract` and `retro` categories collect those
  styles. Three more images moved to a different display format.
- About 40 images were renamed to clearer names, and paired dual-monitor panels
  now end in `-left-panel` and `-right-panel`.
- `wallpapers-desktop.zip` is now `wallpapers-desktops.zip`, desktop
  wallpapers are also offered one category per ZIP, and `wallpapers-source.zip`
  is now `wallpapers-square.zip`.
- Three small images, `hobbit-art`, `multicolor-groot`, and `blue-gradient`,
  were enlarged for current displays. `CREDITS.md` records how.

### Removed

- Three duplicate images from the previous archive.
