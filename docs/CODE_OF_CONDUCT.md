# Code of conduct

Sean's Wallpaper Archive is shared with friends, family, and colleagues.
Everyone who opens an issue, comments, or contributes is expected to keep it a
friendly place.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to GitHub.
- Assume good intentions, and give feedback about the work, not the person.
- Respect artists and photographers: share source and credit information
  honestly, and respect requests from rights holders.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory, hateful, or sexually explicit content, including in
  submitted images.
- Sharing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, or repeated off-topic or disruptive posts.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior concerns you, contact Sean privately using the contact
details on his [GitHub profile](https://github.com/seangalie). Reports are kept
confidential. You can also use GitHub's **Report content** option on any
comment, issue, or pull request.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide, or delete comments; close or lock
conversations; reject contributions; and block people who do not follow this
code of conduct. Responses depend on the situation, from a friendly reminder
to a permanent block for serious or repeated problems.

This applies to all repository spaces, including issues, pull requests,
review comments, and commit messages.
